# Home Run (guinea pig test film, 14.6 s, 120 bpm)

**Logline:** Three guinea pigs want to get home, but the smallest can't keep up, so she takes one giant hop to catch the others, and together they reach the house where a warm door opens for them.

**World:** Sunny meadow turning to golden hour. Palette: soft sky blue, meadow greens, terracotta roof (Clawd clay), cream walls, teal door. Warm tone rises A 0 → B .12 → C .25.

**Motif:** The iris. It opens on the lead pig (A), closes on her (B), opens on her again (C), and closes on her at the end, with the lit door behind.

**Pig arc:** carefree run → small one labours → leader notices → hop, joy → spots house → arrival, panting, happy → hears the door → light → hop for joy.
